Install guide

Two ways to get a face on your wrist.

The Connect IQ Store is the easiest: one tap, color settings, and updates when a face improves. Or download the file and copy it to your watch yourself.

Connect IQ Store
Manual download
RecommendedSettings and updates included.
  1. 1

    Get the Connect IQ app

    Install the Connect IQ Store app on your phone (App Store or Google Play) and sign in with the Garmin account your watch is paired with.

  2. 2

    Install the face

    Tap Connect IQ on any face above to open its store page, or search for the face's name in the app, then tap Install. It syncs to your watch over Bluetooth.

  3. 3

    Pick it and make it yours

    On the watch, hold UP, choose Watch Face, select the new face and press START. To change its colors or cities, open the Connect IQ app, go to My Device, then My Watch Faces, and tap Settings.
